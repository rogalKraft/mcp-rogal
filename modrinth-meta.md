## Environment

Modrinth asks this as a separate step:

- **Client and server → optional for both.**
- Client alone (singleplayer) gives the full toolset. A dedicated server alone gives everything
  except the tools that need a screen. Neither side requires the other, and the two halves do not
  talk to each other.
- Do not pick "required for both", and do not pick the "works better installed on both sides"
  variant — installing on a client and its server on one machine just collides on port 8080.
